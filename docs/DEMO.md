## Likely judge questions
- **"Is this just scraping?"** No. Public posts and public event pages only, and every message is one-to-one and written by the founder. It's built for 10 good conversations a day, not 1,000 spam emails.
- **"Why would a founder pay?"** The bottleneck at 0→100 is founder time. This turns an evening of hunting into 10 minutes a day. Pricing: free for 10 a day, paid for alerts and more sources.
- **"What did you build today vs before?"** Be straightforward about whatever is true.
